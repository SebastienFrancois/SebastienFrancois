# Reel Studio

Drop your rushes and get a vertical reel (9:16) that cuts on the beat, with music, animated text and transitions. It's a single `index.html` file with no server and no install. Everything runs in your browser, so your videos never leave your machine.

**Use it:** open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Drop your videos, press **✨ Auto-edit**, then **⤓ Export reel**.

## What it does

| Step | How |
|---|---|
| **Smart cuts** | Scans each clip (up to 240 samples) for motion, sound level, exposure, contrast and scene changes. It scores every moment and picks the best window for each slot. It avoids windows that span a hard scene change and spreads the picks across your clips. |
| **Rhythm** | Every cut lands on the beat grid. Clip lengths are 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats depending on the pace (Calm / Dynamic / Frenetic). The reel has a structure: an intro bar for the hook, a "drop", a break in the middle of longer reels, and an outro for the call to action. |
| **Music** | 5 built-in music generators, all royalty-free: House, Pop, Trap, Lo-fi, Cinematic. They add a riser and an impact on the drop, and 🎲 Remix changes the key. You can also import your own track: it detects the BPM and the first beat, and starts from the loudest section. |
| **Framing** | Smart crop to 9:16 that follows where the motion is, or a blurred background fill. Ken Burns movement, a zoom pulse on each beat, and slow motion on some long shots. |
| **Transitions** | Auto mix, or one style throughout: cut, zoom punch, flash (on the drop), whip, glitch, fade (on the break). |
| **Text** | A hook in the first seconds. A script where each line becomes a caption, spread across the reel, with words popping in and the current word highlighted. A call to action plus your channel handle at the end. 3 text styles. |
| **Looks** | Natural, Vivid, Warm, Teal & orange, Film, Noir, plus a vignette. |
| **Edit** | The timeline shows every cut. For each one you can edit the caption, change the transition, pick another shot (⟳), reorder or delete it. |
| **Export** | 720p or 1080p. MP4 on recent Chrome and Safari, WebM otherwise. Export records in real time, so keep the tab visible while it runs. |

## Limits

- It doesn't transcribe speech automatically. That would need a speech-to-text service, and today everything stays local. You write captions in the Script field.
- Export takes about as long as the reel lasts (a 30 s reel takes about 30 s).
- Instagram and TikTok want MP4. If your browser only exports WebM, use a recent Chrome (≥ 126) or Safari.
